01 Definitions

  • Quote — the written quote or proposal describing your Project, its price, and its scope.
  • Project — the website build described in the Quote.
  • Site — the website we build for you.
  • Build Fee — the one-time fee for the Project.
  • Launch — the date your Site is first made publicly accessible at your domain.
  • Annual Service — the hosting, domain, and support service described in Section 7.
  • Annual Fee — the recurring yearly fee for the Annual Service.
  • Renewal Date — each anniversary of Launch.

02 Scope

We will build the Site described in your Quote. The Quote is the definition of scope — anything not listed in it (additional pages, new features, e-commerce, booking systems, redesigns, changes of direction after approval) is out of scope.

Out-of-scope work isn't refused, it's re-quoted. We'll tell you before doing anything billable, give you a price, and wait for your go-ahead.

03 Build Fee

The Build Fee is payable in full before we begin, unless your Quote sets out a different schedule.

The Build Fee is non-refundable once work has begun. It reserves your place in our schedule and covers work that starts immediately.

04 Your responsibilities

To keep your Project moving, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate information about your business;
  • Supply your content — text, images, logo, menus, pricing, hours — in a usable format and in reasonable time, or ask us to produce it (quoted separately);
  • Review and approve work at each stage within a reasonable time.

You confirm that everything you give us is yours to use and doesn't infringe anyone else's rights. You are responsible for the accuracy and legality of your content — including prices, claims about your business, professional credentials, and any legally required notices for your industry.

05 Timelines and inactivity

We aim to launch most sites within about two weeks of kickoff. Timelines depend on receiving your content, feedback, and approvals on schedule; delays on either side move the launch date.

If we don't hear from you for 60 consecutive days during an active Project, we'll treat the Project as paused and may close it. The Build Fee is not refunded. If you want to restart later, we'll reinstate the Project subject to our availability and a reinstatement fee.

06 Launch and acceptance

We'll show you the Site for final review before Launch. If you don't raise issues within 7 days of us asking for final approval, the Site is treated as approved and we may launch it.

For two weeks after Launch, we'll fix errors, typos, broken links, and display problems at no charge. After that, changes fall under Section 7 or are quoted separately.

07 Annual Service — what's included

The Annual Service begins at Launch and covers, for one year:

  • Hosting of your Site
  • Domain registration and renewal for one standard domain, where the domain is registered through us
  • SSL certificate and HTTPS
  • Backups and restoration if something goes wrong on our side
  • Platform maintenance — keeping the site's infrastructure current
  • Content updates — one included edit per month. An edit is a request requiring up to one hour of work: text changes, swapping photos, updating hours, prices, menu items, staff, or service listings. Unused edits do not carry forward. Requests are normally handled within 2 business days.

Work beyond the included monthly hour is billed at $70 per hour. If a request looks like it will run past the included hour, we'll tell you and give you an estimate before continuing.

08 What's not included

The following are quoted separately:

  • New pages, new sections, or new features
  • Redesigns or significant restructuring of the Site
  • E-commerce, booking systems, member logins, or custom functionality
  • Copywriting, photography, videography, or logo design
  • SEO campaigns, advertising management, or social media management
  • Third-party subscription costs (booking platforms, payment processors, premium plugins, additional domains)
  • Business email hosting
  • Recovering from problems caused by changes you or a third party make to the Site, domain, or accounts
  • Work required by changes to your legal or regulatory obligations

09 Annual billing and automatic renewal

The Annual Fee is charged automatically each year on the Renewal Date, using the payment method you have on file. The first charge occurs at Launch. You authorize us to store your payment method with our payment processor and to charge it on each Renewal Date without further notice, subject to Section 10.

We'll email you a receipt for each charge. You can update your payment method at any time by contacting us.

Annual Fees are non-refundable. If you cancel, your service continues until the end of the term you've paid for — you don't lose time you've already paid for.

10 Price changes

We may change the Annual Fee. Any increase will be communicated to you by email at least 30 days before it takes effect, and an increase only ever takes effect on a Renewal Date.

This gives you a full two weeks of notice before your cancellation deadline under Section 11. If you don't want to continue at the new price, cancel before that deadline and nothing further is charged.

11 Cancelling the Annual Service

To cancel, email contact@parallexweb.com at least 14 days before your next Renewal Date. We'll confirm your cancellation in writing.

A cancellation received less than 14 days before the Renewal Date takes effect at the following Renewal Date, and the upcoming charge still applies.

When cancellation takes effect:

  • Your Site is removed from our hosting on the Renewal Date;
  • We keep a copy of your Site files for 30 days after that, then delete them;

12 Leaving — what you keep, and what handover costs

Our build pricing is deliberately low. It's set on the assumption of an ongoing hosting and support relationship, and the Build Fee on its own doesn't cover the full value of the work. The Annual Service is what makes that pricing possible. Moving your Site elsewhere also involves real work on our side that isn't covered by fees you've already paid.

So, clearly:

Included at no charge — your files.

Once the Build Fee is paid in full, the finished Site is yours (Section 14). Within 10 business days of your service ending, we'll send you a download link containing your Site's complete source files — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and images — plus any content you originally supplied. We will not withhold these, and we won't hold your Site hostage over a disagreement.

Quoted separately — migration and handover services.

If you want us to do more than hand you the files, that's additional work and we'll quote it before starting. This includes:

  • Setting up and configuring your Site on a new host
  • Transferring or reconfiguring your domain and DNS beyond the standard transfer in Section 13
  • Migrating contact forms, analytics, integrations, or third-party connections
  • Producing documentation, walkthroughs, or technical guidance for you or a new provider
  • Coordinating directly with your new developer or agency
  • Any support during or after the transition

The hosting discount.

Your invoice shows a discount applied to the Build Fee, reflecting a 24-month hosting relationship. That discount earns out over those 24 months — one twenty-fourth of it for each month of Annual Service. Once you've completed 24 months, it's fully yours and nothing further is owed, whenever you choose to move on.

If you move your Site elsewhere before then, we adjust for the part that hasn't been earned yet: the remaining months' share of the discount becomes payable. So if you leave at the end of your first year, half the discount remains, and half is repayable. The full discount amount is printed on your invoice, so the figure is easy to work out at any point.

Nothing is owed under this section if your business ceases operating, or if we end the Agreement ourselves under Section 24.

The domain is yours. If you leave, we will release it or transfer it to a registrar account of your choosing at no charge beyond any registrar transfer cost, provided your account with us is paid in full. We'll do this within 10 business days of your written request. Registrar rules may impose their own waiting periods (typically 60 days after registration or a previous transfer) that are outside our control.

If you already own your domain, you keep it and remain responsible for its renewal.

14 Ownership

Once the Build Fee is paid in full, you own the finished Site created specifically for you — the design and the content we produced for it. Anything you gave us stays yours.

What we keep.

We keep ownership of the underlying templates, components, code patterns, and techniques we reuse across projects, and the right to keep using them. Your ownership is of your finished Site, not of the toolkit it was built with.

Third-party assets.

Fonts, stock photography, icons, and platform features are licensed, not owned. They stay subject to their own licences, which we'll pass to you or set up under your accounts where relevant.
